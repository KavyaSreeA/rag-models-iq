NovaTech Solutions Pvt. Ltd. — Leave Policy

1. Annual Leave: Every full-time employee is entitled to 18 days of paid annual
   leave per calendar year, accrued at 1.5 days per completed month of service.

2. Sick Leave: Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical
   certificate is required for sick leave exceeding 2 consecutive days.

3. Casual Leave: 6 days of casual leave per year for short-notice personal
   needs. Casual leave cannot be combined with more than 3 days of annual leave
   in a single request without manager approval.

4. Maternity Leave: 26 weeks of paid maternity leave, in line with the
   Maternity Benefit Act. Paternity Leave: 15 days of paid leave within 3
   months of the child's birth or adoption.

5. Leave Request Process: All leave requests must be submitted via the HR
   portal at least 3 working days in advance (except sick leave). Requests are
   approved by the employee's direct manager and copied to HR.

6. Unpaid Leave: Employees who exhaust their paid leave balance may request
   unpaid leave, subject to manager and HR approval, for a maximum of 30 days
   per year.

7. Leave Encashment: Up to 5 unused annual leave days may be encashed at the
   end of the calendar year, subject to HR approval.

8. Public Holidays: See the separate Holiday List document for the current
   year's list of company-observed public holidays.
