NovaTech Solutions Pvt. Ltd. — Joining Process

1. Offer & Acceptance: Candidates receive a digital offer letter via the HR
   portal. Acceptance must be confirmed within 7 days.

2. Pre-boarding: Once accepted, new hires complete background verification,
   submit KYC documents (ID proof, address proof, education certificates,
   previous employment relieving letter), and fill in bank details for
   payroll.

3. Day 1: New employees report to the HR desk (or join the virtual onboarding
   session for remote hires) to receive their laptop, ID badge, and system
   access credentials.

4. Orientation: A 2-day orientation program covers company policies, tools,
   security training, and team introductions.

5. Buddy Program: Every new hire is assigned a "buddy" from their team for
   the first 30 days to help them settle in.

6. Probation Period: New hires undergo a 3-month probation period, with a
   formal review at the end of month 3 to confirm employment.

7. Documentation: Employee handbook, IT usage policy, and code of conduct
   must be digitally acknowledged within the first week.
