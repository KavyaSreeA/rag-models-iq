NovaTech Solutions Pvt. Ltd. — Employee Benefits

1. Health Insurance: Group medical insurance covering the employee, spouse,
   and up to 2 children, with a sum insured of INR 5,00,000 per family per
   year. Optional top-up plans are available at employee cost.

2. Provident Fund: 12% of basic salary contributed by both employee and
   employer to EPF, as per statutory requirements.

3. Wellness Allowance: INR 3,000 per quarter reimbursable for gym
   memberships, fitness classes, or mental health counseling sessions.

4. Learning & Development: Annual learning budget of INR 25,000 per
   employee for courses, certifications, and conference attendance, subject
   to manager approval.

5. Employee Referral Bonus: INR 20,000–50,000 depending on role seniority
   for successful referrals who complete 6 months of employment.

6. Meal Benefits: Subsidized meals at office cafeterias; a monthly meal
   allowance for fully remote employees.

7. Life & Accident Insurance: Term life insurance of 3x annual CTC and
   personal accident cover, provided at no cost to the employee.

8. Employee Stock Options (ESOP): Available to employees at Senior level
   and above, vesting over 4 years with a 1-year cliff.
