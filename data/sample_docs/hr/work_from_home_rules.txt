NovaTech Solutions Pvt. Ltd. — Work From Home (WFH) Rules

1. Eligibility: Employees who have completed their 3-month probation period
   and whose role does not require on-site presence are eligible to request a
   WFH arrangement.

2. Hybrid Default: Unless otherwise agreed with a manager, employees follow a
   hybrid schedule of 3 days in-office and 2 days WFH per week.

3. Full Remote: Full-time remote work requires Department Head + HR approval
   and is reviewed every 6 months.

4. Core Hours: WFH employees must be online and reachable during core hours
   (11:00 AM–4:00 PM) and attend all scheduled meetings with camera on unless
   otherwise agreed.

5. Equipment: The company provides a laptop and a one-time home-office setup
   allowance of INR 10,000 for approved full-remote employees.

6. Ad-hoc WFH: Employees may request occasional ad-hoc WFH days (e.g. for a
   home repair) with at least 24 hours' notice to their manager, without
   using a leave day.

7. Data Security: WFH employees must use the company VPN for all work
   involving internal systems and must not use personal devices to access
   confidential company data.
