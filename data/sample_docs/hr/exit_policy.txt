NovaTech Solutions Pvt. Ltd. — Exit Policy

1. Notice Period: Standard notice period is 60 days for confirmed employees
   and 15 days during probation. Notice can be served or bought out per the
   employment contract.

2. Resignation Process: Employees submit resignation via the HR portal.
   Managers acknowledge and initiate a knowledge-transfer plan.

3. Exit Interview: HR conducts a confidential exit interview to gather
   feedback before the employee's last working day.

4. Clearance: IT, Finance, and Admin clearance (asset return, access
   revocation, expense settlement) must be completed before the final
   settlement is processed.

5. Full & Final Settlement: Processed within 45 days of the last working
   day, including pending salary, leave encashment, and any bonus payouts.

6. Relieving & Experience Letter: Issued within 7 working days of successful
   clearance completion.

7. Rehire Eligibility: Employees who leave in good standing are eligible for
   rehire consideration; those exiting for cause are marked ineligible.
