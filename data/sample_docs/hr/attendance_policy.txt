NovaTech Solutions Pvt. Ltd. — Attendance Policy

1. Working Hours: Standard working hours are 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM, Monday to
   Friday, with a 1-hour lunch break. Core collaboration hours are 11:00 AM to
   4:00 PM, during which all employees must be reachable.

2. Attendance Tracking: Employees must clock in/out daily via the company
   attendance system (biometric at office locations, or the mobile app for
   remote/field staff).

3. Late Arrivals: Arriving after 10:00 AM three or more times in a calendar
   month is flagged to the reporting manager. Persistent lateness may result
   in a formal review.

4. Minimum Attendance: Employees must maintain at least 90% attendance
   (present days / working days) per quarter to remain eligible for
   performance bonuses, unless on approved leave.

5. Attendance Percentage Formula:
       attendance_percentage = (days_present / total_working_days) * 100

6. Half Days: Any day with less than 4 hours logged is counted as a half day.
   Two half days are equivalent to one full day of leave deduction.

7. Exceptions: Attendance requirements may be relaxed for employees on
   approved remote work arrangements, subject to manager sign-off (see
   Work From Home Rules).
