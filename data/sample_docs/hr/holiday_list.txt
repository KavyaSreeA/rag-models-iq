NovaTech Solutions Pvt. Ltd. — Company Holiday List (2026)

The following are paid company-wide holidays. Regional/state-specific
holidays may be added per office location — check with your local HR contact.

1. January 1   — New Year's Day
2. January 26  — Republic Day
3. March 6     — Holi
4. March 31    — Eid-al-Fitr (subject to moon sighting)
5. April 14    — Ambedkar Jayanti
6. May 1       — Labour Day
7. August 15   — Independence Day
8. August 28   — Ganesh Chaturthi
9. October 2   — Gandhi Jayanti
10. October 21 — Diwali
11. December 25 — Christmas

Employees observing festivals not listed above may use a Floating Holiday
(2 per year) or a day of casual leave, subject to manager approval.
