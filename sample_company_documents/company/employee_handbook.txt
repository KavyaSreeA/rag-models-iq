NovaTech Solutions Pvt. Ltd. — Employee Handbook (Excerpt)

About NovaTech: NovaTech Solutions Pvt. Ltd. is a software company with more
than 500 employees, delivering enterprise software products across HR,
Operations, Finance, and IT domains. Our mission is to build reliable,
human-centered software that removes repetitive work from our customers'
day-to-day operations.

Code of Conduct: All employees are expected to act with integrity, respect
colleagues and customers, and safeguard confidential company and customer
information. Harassment, discrimination, and conflicts of interest must be
reported to HR immediately via the confidential ethics hotline.

Departments: NovaTech is organized into HR, Operations, Finance, IT Support,
Engineering, Sales, and Administration. Each department has a designated
point of contact listed on the internal wiki.

IT & Security: All employees must use company-issued devices for work,
enable multi-factor authentication, and complete annual security-awareness
training. Report suspected phishing emails to security@novatech.example.

Communication Norms: Slack is used for day-to-day communication, email for
formal/external communication, and the HR portal for policy and leave
requests. Response time expectation for internal Slack messages is same
business day.

Performance Reviews: Conducted twice a year (mid-year and annual), covering
goal achievement, competencies, and development planning. Ratings feed into
compensation review and promotion decisions.

Grievance Redressal: Employees may raise grievances through their manager,
HR business partner, or the anonymous ethics hotline. All grievances are
acknowledged within 3 working days.
