NovaTech Solutions Pvt. Ltd. — IT Support Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

1. Raising a Ticket: All IT issues must be logged via the IT Service Desk
   portal with a category (Hardware, Software, Network, Access) and severity
   (P1–P4).

2. Severity Definitions:
   - P1 (Critical): Production system down, affects all users. Response: 30 min.
   - P2 (High): Major feature broken for a team. Response: 2 hours.
   - P3 (Medium): Individual issue with workaround available. Response: 1 business day.
   - P4 (Low): Minor/cosmetic issue or feature request. Response: 3 business days.

3. Access Requests: New system access requires manager approval via the
   portal; access is provisioned within 1 business day of approval.

4. Password Resets: Self-service password reset is available via the SSO
   portal; IT-assisted resets require identity verification via video call.

5. Equipment Requests: New hardware requests go through the Asset Management
   module and require budget approval from the requesting department head.

6. Software Installation: Only software from the approved catalog may be
   self-installed. Requests for new software require a security review.

7. Escalation: Unresolved P1/P2 tickets past SLA are automatically escalated
   to the IT Manager and, for P1, to the CTO.
